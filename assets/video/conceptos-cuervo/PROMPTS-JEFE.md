# Cuervo: propuestas de jefe final

Generadas con ImageGen integrado. El usuario aclara que el cuervo es el jefe final. Se conserva el tono cómico. La escala junto al mago es orientativa para explorar su presencia, no una decisión de implementación.

Referencias: Escena 1, Escena 4 y los sprites existentes del goblin, gólem y rata.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Design a CROW FINAL BOSS for the user's comic indie game Grimorium. Critical user correction: previous proposals looked like cute mascots or ordinary small enemies. This crow IS THE FINAL BOSS, the arrogant rival who steals the wizard's purple grimoire in the opening. Must command the frame with mass, attitude and a strong distinctive boss silhouette, while remaining funny, simple and intentionally hand-drawn. This is a boss character exploration, NOT a realistic bird, NOT a friendly pet, NOT a tiny baby bird. Avoid horror, demonic themes and a dark tone.
All supplied images are references only. Image 1 shows the protagonist and exact cutscene art: very thick imperfect black marker outlines, ridiculously simple solid color shapes, flat fill with NO shading, children's comic drawing quality. Image 2 shows the old crow stealing the purple grimoire; retain the crow species and stolen purple book, but REPLACE the old realistic anatomy entirely. Image 3 is actual goblin enemy, image 4 actual golem enemy, image 5 rat enemy. Borrow their oversized expressive yellow eyes, squat exaggeration, broad shapes and black outlines. Do NOT upscale or reproduce their pixels. Draw smoothly in the intro's primitive chunky drawing style.
Create a wide single character concept sheet with three clearly separated components: one LARGE main standing boss pose on left taking 60 percent of width; a faithful TINY simple gray-hooded yellow-eyed wizard with cyan-orb staff standing next to its feet solely to demonstrate that the boss is about THREE TIMES THE WIZARD'S HEIGHT and several times his width; one smaller full-body drawing on right of the SAME BOSS flying left with the purple book clutched in its feet, showing that the final boss is recognizable in the intro. Tiny wizard is the only additional character. Both boss poses must preserve its signature body and head proportions. Leave generous margins. The book is a thick simple purple book with pale yellow pages as in the references.
Plain fully OPAQUE solid warm cream background (#eee8d5), not transparent. Flat dark charcoal (#292820) feathers, flat muted slate gray beak, bright yellow eyes with black pupils. At most one additional SOLID gray shape to distinguish wing from torso, no shading. Very heavy slightly irregular black contour all around each shape. Mouth, eyelids and body posture express self-satisfied arrogant comic villain personality. Very simple short blunt feather groups rather than feather detail. At most three large tufts on any contour; easy to draw and animate. NO lighting, NO soft shadows, NO gradients, NO texture, NO rendered 3D, NO naturalistic bird muscles or feathers, NO cute sparkles, NO heroic armor, NO crown, NO robes, NO weapons, NO extra invented lore. Personality and boss scale come from the creature itself. Include a simple black upper-left title exactly as specified.
SPECIFIC BOSS DESIGN: A broad, top-heavy barrel-chested crow boss. Giant squared shoulders made of folded wings held on its hips, a blocky wedge-shaped head sunk into the chest, a large short thick hooked beak like a smug heavy jaw. Small slanted yellow eyes and strong drooping brow built from black contour, one eyebrow lifted as if mocking the wizard. Huge upper body tapering to short sturdy legs and big flat feet. Three very short blunt scalp tufts. His pose leans forward to look down at the tiny mage, with a crooked open-beak smirk. Broad rectangular dominant silhouette: he acts as if he owns the tower. Flight pose keeps his big thick torso and opens enormous chunky blunt wings. Funny bully with pompous authority, not evil horror.
Text (verbatim): "05 · EL MANDÓN".
```
