# Propuestas de cuervo para la intro

Generadas con la herramienta integrada ImageGen. Propuestas para elegir un diseño; sin integrar en el juego.

Referencias: Escena 1, Escena 4 y los sprites existentes de la rata, dragoncito y goblin.

## 01-bolita

Archivo: 01-bolita.png

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Create a comic crow character design proposal for the user's indie game Grimorium, suitable for their hand-drawn opening cutscene. The attached files are STYLE AND STORY REFERENCES, not images to edit. Reference 1 defines the cutscene's deliberately simple imperfect thick black hand-drawn outlines, flat colors and friendly wizard. Reference 2 shows the old crow and purple spellbook theft: replace its realistic long bird anatomy with a completely new comic creature design. References 3, 4 and 5 are actual tiny game enemy sprites (rat, little dragon, goblin); borrow their exaggerated proportions, expressive yellow eyes, comical personality and readable silhouettes, but render as the large smooth hand-drawn cutscene illustration of reference 1, NOT pixel art.
Make one landscape concept sheet with ONLY TWO large full-body drawings of the SAME design. Left: standing three-quarter view showing its face and recognizable silhouette. Right: flying toward the left carrying the purple spellbook from reference 2 in its feet, as if proudly escaping after theft. Keep both drawings uncropped, separated, clear. A single small title at the top. Neutral flat warm off-white background. No wizard, no landscape, no other characters or inset pictures.
Strong amateur indie cartoon charm, intentionally economical shapes. Black heavy slightly wobbly contours, dark charcoal flat body (#292820), a single muted gray flat beak, bright yellow oval eyes with tiny black pupils. Black short feet; purple cover and pale yellow pages on the book. No realistic feather detailing, no anatomical bird study, no long elegant flight feathers, no lighting effects, NO gradients, NO texture, NO shadows, no 3D, no glossy highlights, no polished commercial mascot treatment, no scary or dark mood, no clothes/accessories/hats. Wings and tail simplified to two or three big blunt feather shapes. Funny naughty book-stealing crow that can be redrawn easily and animated in just a few poses.
DESIGN VARIANT: Extremely round, squat ball-shaped body merging into head, almost no neck, very short tiny triangular beak, huge eyes fairly close together, two tiny stubby legs, small stubby wings, a single tiny feather tuft. His heavy round body and disproportionately small wings make the flying theft delightfully absurd. Expression: innocent-looking but smug.
Text (verbatim): "01 · BOLITA".
```
