Use case: background-extraction / precise-object-edit.
Input image is an existing animation frame. Extract ONLY the simplified fat dark crow silhouette from its upper-left area as ONE isolated 2D animation cutout on a genuinely TRANSPARENT background (RGBA alpha).
Keep its exact broad outline: small rounded head with short left-facing crow beak, plump oval belly, two simple swept-up broad wings, short stubby tail, simplified feet. Keep the same level of simplification, no individual feather details. Keep the current flight pose and leftward facing direction. Entire crow should be flat near-black (#121210), with NO interior lines, NO face, eyes, mouth, highlights, texture, gradient or colored regions.
REMOVE EVERYTHING ELSE: no wizard, no landscape, no surprise marks, no purple book, no yellow pages, no staff, no text, no ground, no background, no shadow. Do not add a motion trail, no circular ball, no dramatic feathering. The crow alone, full body and all wing tips and feet uncropped, moderate transparent padding on all sides. This is a practical reusable sprite cutout for an animated intro, not a scene or concept sheet.
